Our Story

Founded in 2018 by master baker Elena Vance, North Star Bakery started as a small neighborhood dream to bring authentic European baking techniques and warm community connection to downtown.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts from the ground up. We partner exclusively with local organic grain farmers and regional dairy producers to ensure sustainable, ethical, and exceptionally flavorful ingredients.

Meet Our Staff

Elena Vance (Founder & Head Baker): With over 20 years of experience across Paris and San Francisco, Elena leads our early-morning dough shaping.

Marcus Chen (Pastry Chef): A master of laminated doughs and delicate seasonal fruit tarts, Marcus brings creativity and precision to our pastry case daily.
